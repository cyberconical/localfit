# Getting started

## The first five minutes

1. **Do the setup.** On first start LocalFit asks for your language, **goal** (5 km, 10 km, half marathon, marathon or general fitness), **level**, race day and an optional goal time, and then for **what you do on each weekday**: runs, strength, bouldering, yoga and so on. It builds your plan. See [[Plan generator|Plan-Generator]].
2. Optionally open Settings (icon at the top right) and enter a **body part you want to watch**, for example your knee. After every run the app then asks how it feels. Leave it empty to turn this off.
3. In the **Training** tab, log your first session or record a run with GPS.
4. Save a **backup** regularly, see [[Backup and data|Backup-and-Data]].

## Defaults of a new installation

- Language **English**; you can switch on the first screen of the setup.
- No plan until the setup is done.
- No watched body part.
- GPS privacy zone of 300 m around the start, route is saved.
- The diagnostic log is on. It contains no personal data, see [[Privacy|Privacy]].

Data from an earlier version (before the language setting existed) opens in English too. Switch to German once under Settings, Language; all entries stay as they are. Backups from the earlier 27-week half marathon app skip the setup and keep their classic plan.

## How the app is organized

| Area | Content |
|---|---|
| **Training** | progress, this week, strength & stability, the whole plan |
| **Habits** | your own habits, streaks, weekly average |
| **Stats** | key figures, charts, map of your routes, weekly overview per sport, learnings |
| **Settings** | language, plan, GPS, diagnostics, records, goals (icon at the top right) |

At the top right there is also the **Save backup** / **All saved** button. The app saves your entries on the device immediately. The button only shows whether something was added since the last backup.
